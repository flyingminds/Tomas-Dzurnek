About Tomas

One craftsman.
One promise.

After twenty-five years on jobsites across Chicagoland, Tomas Dzurnak built his handyman business on the simple idea that every homeowner deserves honest work, fair pricing, and a finish you're proud to show off.

25+
Years on the Tools

I'm Tomas — a general handyman who treats your home the way I'd want someone to treat mine. Over the years I've tackled just about everything: framing basements, tiling showers, building decks, laying wide‑plank hardwood, and pouring mirror‑finish epoxy floors.

What my clients tell me they value most: I'll be straight with you. If I can't do a job well, I'll tell you. If I can, I'll do it right the first time.

Whether it's a weekend punch list or a full bathroom remodel, you'll get the same careful, dust‑swept, respectful service on every visit.

— Tomas Dzurnak Owner · Handyman · Craftsman
